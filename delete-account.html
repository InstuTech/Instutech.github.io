WeEduX Account Deletion Request

Last updated: June 2026

Requesting Account Deletion

WeEduX allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data. If you wish to permanently delete your account, please contact our support team.

Support Contact

Email: instutech.official@gmail.com

Information to Include

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your full name
  • Your school name
  • Your role (Parent, Teacher, or Administrator)

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Authentication account information
  • Push notification tokens
  • Personal information associated with the account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable laws, regulatory obligations, or school administrative requirements.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days of verification of the account owner's identity.
